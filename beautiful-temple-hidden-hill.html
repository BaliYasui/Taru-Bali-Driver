Your day in Bali

Temples, highlands,
and hidden views.

Explore four memorable destinations at a relaxed pace. Departure time and visit duration can be arranged with your local driver.

01
Coastal temple · Tabanan

Pura Tanah Lot

Visit one of Bali's best-known sea temples, set on a rocky outcrop along the coast. Enjoy the ocean scenery and learn about the temple's place in Balinese spiritual life.

02
Lakeside temple · Bedugul

Pura Ulun Danu Beratan

Take in the serene setting of this lakeside temple, framed by the cool highlands and the waters of Lake Beratan. Its distinctive shrines make this a memorable stop for culture and scenery.

03
Highland viewpoint · Wanagiri

Wanagiri Hidden Hill

Pause above the lakes for wide views across Bali's green highlands. Wanagiri Hidden Hill is a scenic spot to enjoy the mountain air and capture the landscapes around the twin lakes.

04
UNESCO-listed landscape · Tabanan

Jatiluwih Rice Terrace

End among the expansive green rice terraces of Jatiluwih. Walk through a landscape shaped by generations of farming and the traditional subak irrigation system.
