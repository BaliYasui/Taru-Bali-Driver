Your day in Ubud

A meaningful day,
made for discovery.

Depart from your hotel at 08:00 am or 09:00 am. You will visit the places below; lunch is not included.

01
Bali tradition

Balinese House Compound

Begin with an introduction to how Balinese people live and what a traditional home looks like. Some compounds have a modern appearance, while others retain a vintage, classic style. Each building has a specific function according to its direction: Bale Daja, Bale Dangin, Bale Delod, and Bale Dauh are the main buildings found in a Balinese house compound.

02
Nature · Ubud

Ubud Monkey Forest

Explore a small tropical rainforest in the heart of Ubud, home to groups of monkeys and other tropical animals. Along with its lush greenery, the forest is a special place to visit temples set within the trees and observe the monkeys in their natural surroundings.

03
Landscape · Tegalalang

Rice Terrace

Bali is famous for its rice paddies, and Tegalalang is one of the most beautiful examples. Its stunning terraces are also known for a unique irrigation system that Balinese farmers have used for centuries.

04
Sacred water · Tampaksiring

Tirta Empul

Locals believe the sacred spring water at Tirta Empul Temple has the power to heal and cleanse the body and soul. Experience the serene atmosphere and history of this important temple, and its sacred spring waters.
