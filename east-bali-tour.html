Your day in East Bali

Island heritage,
with a royal touch.

Visit three of East Bali's best-known cultural landmarks at a relaxed pace. Departure time and visit duration can be arranged with your local driver.

01
Sacred temple · Karangasem

Lempuyang Temple

Visit one of Bali's most revered temple complexes, set high on the slopes of Mount Lempuyang. Take in the mountain scenery, explore the temple grounds respectfully, and learn about its importance in Balinese Hindu tradition.

02
Royal water garden · Karangasem

Tirta Gangga

Stroll through the former royal water palace, known for its pools, fountains, stone sculptures, and tranquil garden setting. Enjoy time to explore the grounds and appreciate this distinctive piece of East Bali's heritage.

03
Water palace · Ujung

Taman Ujung Water Palace

Explore the graceful gardens and historic structures of Taman Ujung, a former royal water palace surrounded by ponds and scenic views. Wander the grounds and take in the architecture and peaceful atmosphere.
