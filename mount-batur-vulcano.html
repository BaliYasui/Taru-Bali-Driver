Your day in Kintamani

A sunrise journey,
at your own pace.

This flexible itinerary can be arranged around your interests and preferred timing. Meal and stop choices can be discussed when planning your day.

01
Sunrise · Adventure

Mount Batur Jeep Tour

Set out by jeep to enjoy the sunrise and the dramatic volcanic scenery around Mount Batur. Take in the changing light and views across the highlands as the day begins.

02
Relax · Kintamani

Café with Volcano and Lake Views

Relax at a café overlooking Mount Batur and Lake Batur. Depending on your request, we can arrange the timing for you to enjoy breakfast or lunch there.

03
Optional · Hot spring

Hot Spring Experience

If you would like, enjoy a relaxing soak at a hot spring in the volcanic area, with beautiful views of the surrounding landscape.

04
Local flavors · Kintamani

Coffee Plantation

On the way back toward your hotel, stop at a coffee plantation or Luwak coffee experience. Kintamani is well known for its coffee-growing areas.

05
Choose your final stop

Tirta Empul Temple or a Waterfall

To finish the day on your return toward the hotel, choose either Tirta Empul Temple or a waterfall, according to your interests.
